About Hackathon

Building India’s Next Generation Hardware

Hosted by the Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering at St. Peter's Engineering College, TEKZITHON 2027 is a prestigious 36-hour national hardware hackathon designed to test practical engineering capability.

From Schematic to Silicon

Unlike software hackathons where virtual code suffices, TEKZITHON 2027 demands physical builds. Teams design circuits, wire PCBs, solder components, write firmware, and validate live hardware sensors.

Industry & Academic Mentorship

Teams receive real-time technical guidance from experienced hardware practitioners and faculty experts across analog design, RF systems, IoT firmware, and robotic kinematics.

Fair & Transparent Evaluation

Submitting your abstract is 100% free. Only shortlisted projects advance to the live build, where prototypes are judged on novelty, feasibility, engineering rigor, and real-world impact.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about submissions, shortlisting, and hackathon participation.

Is abstract submission really 100% free?
Yes, absolutely! Submitting an abstract costs zero rupees. Any eligible student team can submit their project idea and keywords without any payment.
When do we pay the participation fee?
The participation fee of ₹400 per person is payable strictly only after your team has been officially shortlisted. If your abstract is not shortlisted, you do not pay anything.
How is the total team registration fee calculated?
The fee is dynamically calculated at ₹400 per confirmed participant. For a solo participant it is ₹400, for 2 members it is ₹800, for 3 members it is ₹1,200, and for a full team of 4 it is ₹1,600. These are calculated amounts, not a fixed or minimum team fee.
What is the team size?
Team size is 1–4 participants. An individual can register as a team of one, and the team leader counts as one of the participants.
What are the key dates for TEKZITHON 2027?
- Abstract Submission Deadline: 15 December 2026
- Shortlist Announcement: 26 December 2026
- Hackathon Dates: 22 & 23 January 2027 · St. Peter's Engineering College, Maisammaguda, Hyderabad
Where is TEKZITHON 2027 held?
TEKZITHON 2027 takes place on 22 & 23 January 2027 at St. Peter's Engineering College, Maisammaguda, Hyderabad. The Venue section has the Google Maps link. For other details, write to ecehackathon@stpetershyd.com.

TEKZITHON 2027 – Rules & Regulations

36-Hour National-Level Hardware Hackathon

20 rules · 4 important instructions

Rules and Regulations for TEKZITHON 2027

  1. 1

    Participation is open to students from recognized engineering and technical institutions.

  2. 2

    Each team must consist of a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 4 members.

  3. 3

    Participants must register through the official registration form.

  4. 4

    Each team must submit an abstract describing the proposed hardware project on or before 15 December 2026.

  5. 5

    Abstract submission is free of cost. Only shortlisted teams will be invited for final registration.

  6. 6

    Shortlisted teams must complete final registration and verification on 26 December 2026.

  7. 7

    A participation fee of ₹400 per participant applies to shortlisted teams.

  8. 8

    Each team must provide the details of all participating members, including their names, department, year of study, institution, email addresses, and contact numbers.

  9. 9

    Teams must work on projects related to the listed hackathon domains or propose an innovative idea under the Open Innovation category.

  10. 10

    Projects must be original. Plagiarism, copied designs, and unauthorized use of other teams' work are strictly prohibited.

  11. 11

    Participants must bring their own laptops, development boards, sensors, electronic components, tools, and other required hardware.

  12. 12

    Teams must complete their prototypes within the 36-hour hackathon period.

  13. 13

    Participants must follow the instructions provided by the organizing committee and faculty coordinators.

  14. 14

    All participants must maintain discipline and follow the rules and regulations of the institution throughout the event.

  15. 15

    Any damage to college property or laboratory equipment caused by negligence will be the responsibility of the concerned participants.

  16. 16

    Each team must present a working prototype and explain the design, implementation, functionality, and applications of their project before the evaluation panel.

  17. 17

    Projects will be evaluated based on innovation, technical implementation, functionality, practical applicability, problem-solving approach, and presentation.

  18. 18

    The decision of the judging panel and organizing committee will be final.

  19. 19

    Participants must not engage in misconduct, unfair practices, or activities that disrupt the event.

  20. 20

    The organizing committee reserves the right to modify the event schedule or rules whenever necessary.

IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS

  • Registration does not guarantee shortlisting.
  • Only shortlisted teams are eligible to participate in the final 36-hour hackathon.
  • All participants must report to the venue as per the schedule communicated by the organizing committee.
  • Participants must carry their valid college ID cards during the event.
